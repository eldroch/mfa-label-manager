Make Trello's own label list follow this order (optional, advanced)

Trello's built-in picker and card chips always sort by a fixed color sequence (subtle → normal → bold green, then yellow, orange, red, purple, blue, sky, lime, pink, black) — no Power-Up can change that screen directly. Two workarounds exist, both of which really modify your labels:

1 · Number prefixes — renames labels like “2· Bug” so that name sorting reproduces your order within each color group. (Trello has never documented the within-color tie-break; community testing indicates it is by name, but verify on your own board.) Automations that match label names may need updating.

2 · Recolor to match — reassigns colors following Trello's palette sequence so your first 30 labels appear in exactly your custom order everywhere in Trello. This overwrites every affected label's color (your previous colors are backed up so you can undo), and colorless labels will gain a color, making them visible on card fronts.
